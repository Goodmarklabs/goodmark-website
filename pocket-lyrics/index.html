Your songbook, in your key, on every stage.

Pocket Lyrics is a chord and lyric songbook for musicians. Type in the songs you play, and read them from your phone or tablet with the chords sitting right above the words.

Coming soon to Android and iOS Support

Everything a working musician needs, nothing else

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app stores your email address and your songs so they can sync, and nothing more.

  • Chords over lyrics

    Chords sit exactly where they are played, laid out cleanly at any text size.

  • Transpose

    Move any song to a new key in a tap. The original is always kept.

  • Sets

    Order songs for a gig or a service and choose a key for each one.

  • Chord detection

    Hear the chords in a recording or from the microphone, processed on your device.

  • Tuner

    A guitar tuner built in, so you never leave the songbook.

  • Chord charts

    Look up any chord in your songs and see how to play it.

  • Sync

    One account keeps your songbook the same on every device.

  • Works offline

    Your songs live on the device. No signal needed on stage.

  • Import and export

    ChordPro (.cho) and plain text in and out. Your songs are never locked in.
