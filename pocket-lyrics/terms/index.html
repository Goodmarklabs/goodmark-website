1. What Pocket Lyrics is

Pocket Lyrics is a personal songbook. You type or paste in the lyrics and chords of songs you play, keep them in your own library, arrange them into sets, and read them on stage from your phone, tablet or computer. It also includes a guitar tuner, a chord library and a chord detection feature. Pocket Lyrics does not supply any lyrics or chords itself: everything in your library is put there by you.

5. Microphone and audio features

The Tuner and Detect features use your device's microphone, and Detect can open audio files you choose. All of this is processed on your device and nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded (see the Privacy Policy). You control microphone access in your device settings.

Chord detection, key detection and the tuner are automatic estimates. They can be wrong, especially with complex music or in a noisy room. They are a helpful starting point, not a substitute for your ears or for professional transcription.
